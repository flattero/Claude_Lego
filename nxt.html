Connection help

USB: plug the brick into the computer with its USB cable. On Windows, if the brick is in the list but connecting fails, Windows is using LEGO's own driver. Use the free tool Zadig to switch the "NXT" device to the WinUSB driver. Note: the old LEGO NXT software can't see the brick after that until you switch it back.

Bluetooth: on the brick, go to Bluetooth → On/Off → On, and Visibility → Visible. Pair the brick in the computer's Bluetooth settings first (the passkey is usually 1234; confirm it on the brick with the orange button). Then click Connect by Bluetooth and choose the brick or its COM port.

Which COM port? (Windows) Windows creates two ports for the brick. Open Settings → Bluetooth & devices → More Bluetooth settings → COM Ports, and use the one marked Outgoing with the brick's name.

Worked once, now fails? The NXT's Bluetooth can get stuck after a connection ends. Turn the brick off and on, and make sure no other tab or program (such as the LEGO software) is connected to it. If it still fails, remove the brick in the computer's Bluetooth settings and pair it again.
